## Why Cybersecurity?

It started with video games. My love for them got me into tech, and when I sat down and really thought about what I wanted to do with my life, tech was the only thing that kept pulling me back. I wanted a job where I keep learning cool things, see how it all works under the hood, fix the problems people run into, and build tools I'm proud to show off. Basically, I want to be the person who knows exactly what they're doing with a PC.

Cybersecurity is where all of that comes together. It's the part of tech where understanding how things work matters most: how systems fit together, where they break, and how to protect them. It's also problem-solving all day, which is exactly the kind of work I want to be doing. So I'm building the skills to get there.
